## Revenue and segment performance

Structured evidence shows TTM revenue of 451.4B. Exhibit 99.1 reports March-quarter revenue of 111.2B, up 17% year over year, and describes records for total company revenue, iPhone revenue, EPS, and services revenue. Exhibit 99.1 also reports product net sales of 80.208B and services net sales of 30.976B for the quarter. (Source: structured fundamentals packet; 8-K Exhibit 99.1)

## Margin and profitability trend

Apple shows high profitability in the structured packet: net income 122.6B, profit margin 27.15%, operating margin 32.28%, and ROE 1.41. Exhibit 99.1 reports diluted EPS of 2.01, up 22% year over year, and includes the condensed statement of operations that supports margin review. (Source: structured fundamentals packet; 8-K Exhibit 99.1)

## Cash flow quality

Free cash flow is 101.1B in the structured packet. Exhibit 99.1 states the March quarter generated over 28B in operating cash flow and set March-quarter records for operating cash flow and EPS. This supports strong cash-generation quality. (Source: structured fundamentals packet; 8-K Exhibit 99.1)

## Capex and investment commitments

The 10-K liquidity section reports cash, cash equivalents, and marketable securities of 132.4B as of September 27, 2025, debt obligations, lease obligations, manufacturing purchase obligations of 56.2B, and other purchase obligations of 14.8B. The 10-Q liquidity section updates manufacturing purchase obligations to 44.6B and other purchase obligations to 30.4B as of March 28, 2026. This provides a stronger basis for discussing supplier and manufacturing commitments than the prior ratio-only packet. (Source: 10-K liquidity and capital resources; 10-Q liquidity and capital resources; 10-Q commitments / capex / contractual obligations)

## Balance sheet and liquidity

Current ratio is 1.07 and debt/equity is 79.55. The 10-Q liquidity section says Apple believes cash, marketable securities, operating cash generation, and access to debt markets will be sufficient for cash requirements and the capital return program over the next 12 months and beyond. It also reports remaining share-repurchase authorization of 63.8B as of March 28, 2026 before the additional 100B program announced April 30, 2026. (Source: structured fundamentals packet; 10-Q liquidity and capital resources; 8-K Exhibit 99.1)

## Management guidance / outlook

The 8-K cover page confirms the press release was furnished as Exhibit 99.1, and Exhibit 99.1 includes management commentary from the CEO and CFO. The CEO attributed the record March quarter to total revenue, iPhone demand, services, and new products; the CFO cited operating cash flow, EPS, and installed-base strength. The press release includes forward-looking risk language but does not provide a numerical formal guidance table in the extracted excerpt. (Source: 8-K cover page; 8-K Exhibit 99.1)

## Material risk factors

The extracted 10-K and 10-Q risk-factor sections are available and should be used for formal risk language. Exhibit 99.1 also points readers to risk factors and MD&A in SEC filings. Evidence-supported risks remain premium valuation, memory/input-cost pressure, app-store regulatory pressure in Brazil, AI roadmap credibility, and supplier approval risk around blacklisted Chinese memory. (Source: 10-K risk factors; 10-Q risk factors; 8-K Exhibit 99.1)
